廈門國際銀行成立於1985年，是中國首家中外合資銀行。目前境內股東為中國工商銀行、福建投資企業集團公司和廈門建發集團有限公司，境外股東包括閩信集團有限公司、亞洲開發銀行、日本新生銀行和美國賽諾金融集團有限公司。

廈門國際銀行總行設於廈門經濟特區，在香港、澳門分別擁有附屬機構——廈門國際投資有限公司和澳門國際銀行股份有限公司（下轄10家分行），在北京、上海、福州、珠海、廈門、泉州等地區設有16家營業性機構（其中2家籌建中）和1家代表處，同時與遍佈60多個國家和地區的約300家銀行建立了代理行關係，全球化服務網絡日益擴大和完善。

目前，廈門國際銀行已獲得全面外匯業務、對公人民幣業務、衍生產品交易業務等多項業務牌照，可以為個人客戶提供全面外匯業務，為企業客戶和非居民客戶提供包括人民幣業務在内的全面金融服務。

廈門國際銀行堅持“經營智慧”的核心經營理念，奉行“以人為本、創新求變、開拓進取、團結奉獻”的企業文化核心價值觀，堅持國際化、標準化、集約化與精細化的經營管理導向，不斷拓寬戰略創新平臺，加強核心信息系統建設，加快夯實管理基礎；實行“拾遺補缺”的市場策略，打造靈活應變的業務運作機制，建設因應客戶和市場需求的彈性組織體系，通過創新金融產品和個性化方案，大力發展優質的基礎客戶，實現業務的可持續發展；推行全面風險管理，形成科學有效的風險控制體系，及時化解市場突變帶來的潛在風險，確保資產質量保持優秀水平。

廈門國際銀行在實現自身發展的同時，熱心參與各種公益事業回饋社會。積極支持教育事業，設立獎教和助學基金，資助邊遠學校完善教育設施，幫助困難學生完成學業；關心弱勢群體，為福利院配備生活設施，組織向貧困家庭捐款捐物；大力支持環保事業，推行“綠色信貸”，認釐數百畝林地，定期組織義務植樹；推廣感恩文化，鼓勵員工自動自發地投身公益事業，踴躍參加志願者行動、無償獻血等各類公益活動。廈門國際銀行在踐行企業社會責任方面做出了積極的努力，得到了社會各界的廣泛讚譽。

作為第一家地跨港澳和大陸的中外合資銀行，廈門國際銀行經過20多年的精耕細作，已經成為一家在國內外享有良好聲譽的銀行集團，連續多年入選英國《銀行家》雜誌評選的“全球銀行1000強”，榮獲全國“外商投資雙優企業”、“企業文化建設優秀單位”、“最佳人力資源典範企業”、地方“納稅大戶”、“利稅100強”、“納稅信用等級A級企業”（最高等級）、“文明單位”等多項榮譽稱號。

Established in 1985, Xiamen International Bank (XIB) is the first joint venture bank in China. Its shareholders include domestic shareholders such as Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, Fujian Investment and Enterprise Holdings Corporation, Xiamen C&D Corporation Limited, as well as overseas shareholders including Min Xin Holdings Limited, Asian Development Bank, Shinsei Bank, Limited (Japan) and Sino Finance Group Limited, U. S. A.

Based in Xiamen, XIB operates subsidiaries including Xiamen International Investment Limited (XIIL) in Hong Kong and Luso International Banking Limited (LIB) which runs ten branches in Macau. In Chinese Mainland, XIB operates sixteen establishments (including two under preparation) and one representative office in cities of Beijing, Shanghai, Fuzhou, Zhuhai, Xiamen, Quanzhou, etc. So far, the Bank has established correspondent relationships with nearly 300 banks from more than 60 countries and regions, with its global service network gradually expanding.

At present, XIB has been officially granted the licenses on full-range foreign exchange business operation and corporate RMB business operation as well as the qualification for financial derivatives dealing, which entitle the Bank to offer not only full-range foreign exchange business for personal customers, but also comprehensive business (including RMB business) for corporate customers and offshore personal customers.

XIB pursues the philosophy of "operating intelligence" as its guiding principle, and regards "recognizing human merits, advocating innovations, development and assertiveness, cultivating the spirit of teamwork and dedication" as the soul of its corporate culture. XIB adheres to the orientation of internationalized, standardized, integrated and refined management. It continuously strengthens innovation, enhances the building of core banking system, and accelerates the consolidation of management basis. Contributing to the distinguished operation features of XIB are its market positioning strategy of "sticking to niche market", its efforts made on building flexible and efficient operation mechanism, elastic organization framework catering to the demands of the customers and markets, and the marketing strategy characterized by innovative products as well as tailor-made solutions which build high-quality customer basis, and the comprehensive risk management which features scientific and effective risk control mechanism for timely reducing the potential risks brought by the sudden market changes so as to ensure an outstanding asset quality.

XIB not only dedicates to self-development but also contributes to public welfare. The Bank actively supports education by setting up education funds for teachers and students, making donations to schools in the remote areas to improve their school facilities, as well as providing assistance to students in poverty. XIB also cares for the disadvantaged by providing living facilities to Xiamen Welfare Institution, and by donating money or relief materials to poor families. XIB energetically advocates environmental protection and "green-credit policy" which restricts loans facilities to high-pollution industries. XIB also adopts several hundred mu of forest land, and organizes tree planting on a regular basis. The culture of "being thankful" is promoted by encouraging the staff to contribute to the public welfare, and to participate in volunteer activities, blood donation, etc. In all, XIB has made active efforts in fulfilling its social responsibilities, and is widely esteemed by the public.

As the first Sino-foreign joint-venture bank operating across Hong Kong, Macau and the Chinese Mainland, through decades of development, XIB has grown into a bank enjoying good reputation home and abroad. For several consecutive years, the Bank has been listed among The Banker's "Top 1000 Banks Worldwide". The Bank has also earned several honors including the "National Double Excellence Enterprise with Foreign Capital", "Excellent Enterprise in Cultural Building", "Best Model Enterprise in Human Resources Management", the local "Major Tax-payers" and the "Top 100 Tax-payers of Fujian Province", "Grade A (the highest grade) Enterprise in Tax-paying Credit Rating", "Model Enterprise in Ethical and Cultural Progress", etc.

二零一零年三月二十五日

2009 年，全球經濟經歷了衰退、止跌、回穩的曲折過程。面對國內外經濟形勢變化的嚴峻考驗，本行貫徹“誠信為本、經營智慧、駕馭風險、精藝管理”的戰略思想，執行“靈活應變，優化結構，提升效率，借勢發展”的年度工作方針，堅持以效益為中心，全力防範和化解各類風險，大力拓展基礎客戶，調整優化各項結構，順利度過了全球金融危機後最困難的一年。

經過管理層和全體員工堅持不懈的努力，2009年，全行各項業務保持穩健發展，年末存、貸款總額分別比上年末增長7.54%和2.31%，總資產比上年末增長6.60%；盈利水平穩步提升，稅後利潤比上年增長11.79%，稅後股東權益收益率和稅後資產收益率分別達到15.81%和0.91%；資產質量繼續保持優秀水平，不良貸款率和不良資產率分別為0.63%和0.38%。

2009 年，本行各項重點工作取得實質性進展，對實現本行中長期發展目標具有深遠意義。本行調整優化了組織流程，為打造流程銀行做好鋪墊；順利完成櫃面系統升級等重大信息系統建設，促進運作效率和質量的提升；加快推進新一代信息系統建設，為未來快速發展奠定高起點的軟、硬件平臺基礎；獲准籌建和設立多家同城支行，完善營業網點佈局，擴大客戶服務半徑；深入開展第三個五年規劃研究工作，積極探索和論證銀行中長期發展方向。

本人謹代表董事會向辛勤付出的管理層和全體員工表示衷心的感謝，並向關心和支持本行發展的各級監管部門、政府部門、廣大客戶和社會各界致以誠摯的謝意！

2010 年，國內外經濟正企穩復蘇。本行將繼續審時度勢，把握歷史機遇，加快銀行改革步伐，致力於為客戶提供優質的金融服務，為員工搭建廣闊的發展平臺，為股東創造良好的回報，迎接新的飛躍。

In 2009, the world economy experienced a complicated process, from recession, to stop falling and restore stability. Facing the stringent external environment both home and abroad, by adhering to the strategic principles of "establish integrity as the basis of development, manage with wisdom, manage risks, and enhance profound management", and following the working guidelines of the year, which is to be flexible and adaptable, to improve structure, to enhance efficiency, and to realize development by seizing the opportunities, our bank has been focusing on operating efficiency, preventing and resolving various risks, consolidating our customer base, as well as perfecting various structures, and has successfully passed the most difficult year following the global financial crisis.

Through the concerted efforts by the Management and the whole staff, we witnessed stable growth in various businesses. The year-end deposits and loans outstanding rose by 7.54% and 2.31% respectively against the last year end, while the total assets was up by 6.60%. Our profitability continued to improve, with after-tax profit rose by 11.79% against last year, and after-tax ROE and ROA reaching 15.81% and 0.91% respectively. The asset quality was maintained at a fine level, with non-performing loan ratio and non-performing asset ratio at 0.63% and 0.38% respectively.

In 2009, our bank has made substantial progress on various major tasks which are of far-reaching influences on the achievement of our medium and long-term development goals. Our bank has adjusted and optimized the organizational processes, paving the way to the building of process banking. The building of major information systems were successfully completed, including the upgrade of Counter System, significantly enhancing our operational efficiency and quality. The construction of the new generation information system was sped up with an aim to prepare high standard software and hardware platforms for our rapid development in the future. In addition, several sub-branches have been approved to be established or be prepared, which further perfected our branching network and expanded our customer service radius. Our bank also conducted in-depth research into the Third Five-Year Plan, and actively explored and discussed the long-term development direction of our bank.

On behalf of the Board, I hereby express sincere thanks for the strenuous endeavors of the management and the whole staff, and extend our heartfelt gratitude to regulatory authorities and government bodies at various levels, as well as to our customers and all walks of life, who have given valuable care and supports to the development of our bank!

In 2010, the economies at home and abroad are stabilizing for recovery. Our bank will continue to carefully size up the situation and grasp the historical opportunities, and to accelerate the pace of our reform. We commit to provide quality financial services to customers, to build broader development platform for our employees, and to create good returns for our shareholders, and are ready to meet new leaps ahead.

## Ding Shi Da

Chairman
