##### 銀行簡介

廈門國際銀行成立於1985年8月，是中國首家中外合資銀行，注册资本10.69億元人民幣，現有股東包括中國工商銀行、福建投資企業集團公司、廈門建發集團有限公司、聞信集團有限公司、亞洲開發銀行、日本新生銀行及美國賽諾金融集團有限公司。

廈門國際銀行總部設在中國廈門，在香港、澳門分別設有附屬機構——廈門國際投資有限公司和澳門國際銀行股份有限公司（下轄11家分行），在國內設有上海分行、福州分行（下轄福州鼓樓支行）、珠海分行、廈門直屬支行（下轄東區支行、嘉禾支行）和泉州代表處，並已獲准正在籌建北京分行。目前銀行與分佈在60多個國家和地區的170多家中外銀行建立了代理行關係，商務聯繫遍及全球。

虞門國際銀行堅持“經營智慧”的核心經營理念，確立“以人為本、創新求變、開拓進取、團結奉獻”的企業文化核心價值觀。銀行推行“拾遺補缺”的市場營銷策略，打造激活、高效的業務運作機制，形成因應客戶和市場需求的彈性組織體系，實施以創新為本的業務拓展策略，推出本外幣結合、醍在岸結合、境內外結合等各種組合型產品以及個性化方案；實行全面風險管理，形成科學的風險控制體系；以國際化、標準化、集約化與扁平化為導向，持續勞實管理基礎。

經過20多年的發展，廈門國際銀行已成長為一家在國內外享有良好豐譽的銀行，連續多年入選英國《銀行家》雜誌“世界1000大銀行”和“亞洲200大銀行”，多次榮獲全國“外商投資雙優企業”、福建省“納稅大戶”、“利稅300強”等榮譽稱號。在廈門市國視局和地視局聯合評審的納稅信用等級中，廈門國際銀行獲得最高等級“A級企業”的稱號。

廈門國際銀行在自身獲得發展的同時，不忘參加各種公益事業回饋社會，培育員工的感恩之心，如賴鐘員工向福利院提供無私捐贈、參加無償獻血、參加植樹造林等公益活動，得到了社會各界的廣泛贊譽，被福建省評價協會授予“熱心公益企業”榮譽稱號，並連續榮獲“廈門市文明單位”、“廈門市職工職業道德建設先進集體”、“企業文化建設優秀單位”、“廈門市‘區人獻愛心’活動先進單位”等稱號。

Established in August 1985, Xiamen International Bank (XIB) is the first joint venture bank in China with a paid-in capital of RMB1.069 billion. Its shareholders include Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, Fujian Investment & Enterprise Holdings Corporation, Xiamen C&D Corporation Limited, Min Xin Holdings Limited (a Hong Kong listed company), Asian Development Bank, Shinsei Bank Limited (from Japan) and Sino Finance Group Limited, U.S.A.

Based in Xiamen, XIB operates Xiamen International Investment Limited (XIIL) in Hong Kong and Luso International Banking Limited (LIB) which runs eleven branches in Macau. Both XIIL and LIB are subsidiaries of XIB. In Chinese Mainland, XIB operates five major establishments including Shanghai Branch, Fuzhou Branch which also runs Fuzhou Gulou Sub-branch, Zhuhai Branch, Xiamen Sub-branch which also runs Dongqu Sub-branch and Jiahe Sub-branch in Xiamen, and Quanzhou Representative Office. And the bank has been approved to prepare for establishing Beijing Branch, So far, the Bank has established correspondent relations with 170 domestic and foreign banks from more than 60 countries and regions, with business links all over the world.

XIB persistently pursues the philosophy of 'operating intelligence' as its guiding principle, and nurtures the soul of corporate culture as 'recognizing human merits, advocating innovations, development and assertiveness, cultivating the spirit of teamwork and dedication'. 'Creating niche market' serves as its marketing strategy and strenuous efforts are made on building flexible and efficient operation mechanism and elastic organization framework catering to the demands of the customers and markets. The bank's innovative business exploration strategy is characterized by portfolio products as well as tailor-made solutions which integrate RMB and foreign exchange business, onshore and offshore business, domestic and international business. Contributing to the formation of advanced risk-control system is the implementation of comprehensive risk management. The bank consistently fortifies the management foundation by keeping pace with the international practices, striving for standard operation and centralized processing, and constructing a flat organization framework for the bank.

Through over two decades of development, XIB has grown into a bank enjoying good reputation home and abroad. For several consecutive years, the bank has been listed among The Bankers' "Top 1000 Banks Worldwide" and "Top 200 Asian Banks". The Bank has also earned several honors including the "National Double Excellence Enterprise with Foreign Capital", the "Major Tax-payers" and the "Top 300 Tax-payers" of Fujian Province. In the tax-paying credit rating program jointly held by Xiamen State Taxation Bureau and Local Taxation Bureau, XIB is rated Grade A Enterprise (the highest grade).

When dedicating to self-development, XIB also contributes to public welfare and makes hard efforts to cultivate a culture of appreciation and thanksgiving among its staff. The Bank is highly esteemed for its donation to Xiamen Welfare Institution and for organizing public welfare activities including blood donations and tree planting. Due to its impressive contribution to the public welfare, the bank has been granted the honor of "Enterprise Active in Public Welfare" by Fujian Assessment Society, as well as "Civilized Corporation in Xiamen", "Corporation of Excellent Professional Ethic in Xiamen", "Corporation of Excellent Corporate Culture" and "Excellent Performance Award for Public Welfare in Xiamen".

2007年，面對中國金融業全面開放的機遇與挑戰，本行將進一步推進各項改革措施，潭植根基，成就未來，以優异的業績回報股東、泰獻社會、造福員工

In 2006, pursuing the strategic guidelines of "establishing integrity as the basis of development, managing with wisdom, reining risks and advocating profound management", our bank started in full force the implementation of the Second Five-Year Plan. Efforts were maintained on market development and innovation, meanwhile the business structure was further optimized, the internal control was further enhanced, the restructuring of organization and processes was carried out as scheduled, the compliance management system was reinforced and the asset-liability management was steadily promoted. By all these measures, our bank witnessed a favorable opening year of the Second Five-Year Plan.

In the year, the businesses of our bank maintained the trend for high-quality, efficient and sustainable growth, with our profitability went up persistently and asset quality further improved. By the end of the year, the deposits and outstanding loans gained an increase of 27.21% and 26.36% respectively compared to the previous year, and the total assets hit a record high of over RMB30 billion, 31.15% up against last year. The after-tax profits exceeded RMB280 million, increasing 39.98% against that of last year. The after-tax ROE numbered at 16.45% and after-tax ROA stood at 1.05%, at the same time, the NPA ratio further decreased to 0.49%.

In 2006, our bank also witnessed favorable progress in several important aspects. We took a crucial step forward in branching activities by obtaining the official approval for preparing for establishment of Beijing Branch, which symbolized the nationwide marketing network of our bank has taken shape. The new credit approval system was put into force, laying a foundation for centralized, professional and efficient credit and investment approval process. The new-generation IT system construction was launched, leading us towards a modern IT-driven bank.

In 2007, in the face of the opportunities and challenges brought about by the deregulation of the financial sector in China, our bank will continue to push forward reform in various aspects and fortify our basis to guarantee a promising future. With excellent performance, our bank will redound on the shareholders, contribute to the society and benefit our staff!

## 致：廈門國際銀行

我們計了後附的慶門國際銀行及其合併子公司（以下簡稱“慶門國際銀行”）合併計報表，包括2006年12月31日合併資產負債表、2006年度合併利潤及利潤分配表、合併現金流量表以及會計報表附注。

## 一、管理層對合併會計報表的責任

按照企業會計事則和中華人民共和國財政部於2001年11月27日印發的《金融企業會計制度》的規定編制合併會計報表是慶門關照銀行管理層的責任。這種責任包括：（1）設計、實施和細選與合併會計報表編制相關的內部控制，以使合併會計報表不存在由於舞弊或錯誤而導致的重大錯報；（2）選擇和運用給當的會計政策；（3）作出合理的會計估計。

我們的責任是在實施書計工作的基礎上對合併會計報表發表書計意見。我們按照中國註冊會計師書計準則的規定執行了書計工作。中國註冊會計師書計準則要求我們遵守職業道德規範，計劃和實施書計工作以對合併會計報表是否不存在重大錯誤徵取合理保證。

## 二、注册会计师的责任

審計工作涉及實施審計程序，以獲取有關合併會計報表金額和披露的審計證據。選擇的審計程序取決於註冊會計師的判斷，包括對由於舞弊或錯誤導致的合併會計報表重大錯報風險的評估。在進行風險評估時，我們考慮與合併會計報表編制相關的內部控制，以設計給當的審計程序，但目的並非對內部控制的有效性發表意見。審計工作還包括評情管理層選用會計政策的恰當性和作出會計估計的合理性，以及評價合併會計報表的總體列報。

我們相信，我們獲取的審計證據是充分、適當的，將發表審計意見提供了基礎。

## 三、审计意见

我們認爲，廈門國際銀行上述合併會計報表已經按照企業會計基則和中華人民共和國财政部於2001年11月27日印發的《金融企業會計制度》編制，在所有重大方面公允反映了廈門國際銀行2006年12月31日的財務狀況以及2006年度的經營成果和現金流量。

Management is responsible for the preparation of these financial statements in accordance with the Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises and the 'Accounting System for Financial Institution'. This responsibility includes: designing, implementing and maintaining internal control relevant to the preparation of financial statements that are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error; selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies; and making accounting estimates that are reasonable in the circumstances.

#### Auditor's Responsibility

#### Opinion

Our responsibility is to express an opinion on these financial statements based on our audit. We conducted our audit in accordance with China Auditing Standards. Those standards require that we comply with ethical requirements and plan and perform the audit to obtain reasonable assurance whether the financial statements are free from material misstatement.

An audit involves performing procedures to obtain audit evidence about the amounts and disclosures in the financial statements. The procedures selected depend on the auditor's judgment, including the assessment of the risks of material misstatement of the financial statements, whether due to fraud or error. In making those risk assessments, the auditor considers internal control relevant to the entity's preparation of the financial statements in order to design audit procedures that are appropriate in the circumstances, but not for the purpose of expressing an opinion on the effectiveness of the entity's internal control. An audit also includes evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies used and the reasonableness of accounting estimates made by management, as well as evaluating the overall presentation of the financial statements.

We believe that the audit evidence we have obtained is sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for our audit opinion.

In our opinion, the financial statements present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position of Xiamen International Bank as of 31 December 2006, and of its financial performance and its cash flows for the year then ended in accordance with Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises and the 'Accounting System for Financial Institution'.
