廈門國際銀行成立於1985年8月，是中國首家中外合資銀行，註冊資本人民幣10.69億元。現有股東包括中國工廠銀行、福建投資企業集團公司、廈門建發集團有限公司、閩信集團有限公司、亞洲開發銀行、日本新生銀行及美國賽諾金融集團有限公司。

廈門國際銀行總部設在中國廈門，在香港、澳門分別擁有附屬機構——廈門國際投資有限公司和澳門國際銀行（下載10家分行），在國內設有上海分行、福州分行、珠海分行、廈門直屬支行和東區支行以及泉州代表處，並正積極響建聽州鼓樓支行和廈門嘉禾支行。目前與分佈在59個國家和地區的170多家中外銀行建立代理行關係，商務聯繫遍及全球。

目前，廈門國際銀行已經獲得全面外匯業務和人民幣業務牌照以及衍生產品交易業務資格，可以為個人客戶提供全面外匯業務，為企業客戶和非居民提供包括人民幣業務在内的全面金融服務。

“經營智慧”是廈門國際銀行的核心經營理念，“以人為本、創新求變、開拓進取、團結泰獻”是本行企業文化的精髓所在。本行推行拾遺補缺的市場營銅模式，打造體活、高效的業務運作機制，形成因應客戶和市場需求的彈性組織體系，實施以創新為本的業務拓展策略，推出本外幣結合、趨在岸結合、境內外結合等各種組合型產品以及個性化方案，初步形成了鮮明的經營特色。

經過多年的發展，廈門國際銀行已成長為一家地跨港澳和大陸、在國內外享有良好聲譽的銀行，連續多年入選英國《銀行家》雜誌評選的“世界1000大銀行”，在2005年度“亞洲200大銀行”評選中排名第167位，多次榮獲全國“外碼投資雙優企業”、福建省“納稅大戶”、“利稅300强”。在廈門市國稅局和地稅局聯合評審的納稅信用等級中，本行獲得最高等級“A級企業”的稱號。

廈門國際銀行在自身發展的同時，不忘回饋社會。每年都組織員工看望邊邊山區的希望小學師生，向實困山區學校捐資助學，向福利院提供無私捐贈，積極參加無償獻血、植樹造林等公益活動。這些活動得到了社會各界的廣泛讚譽，本行被福建省評價協會授予“熱心公益企業”獎譽稱號，並榮獲“廈門市文明單位”、“廈門市職工職業道德建設先進集體”、“企業文化建設優秀單位”等稱號。

Established in August 1985, Xiamen International Bank (XIB) is the first joint venture bank in China with a registered capital of RMB1.069 billion. Its shareholders include Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, Fujian Investment and Enterprise Holdings Corporation, Xiamen C&D Corporation Limited, Min Xin Holdings Limited (Hong Kong listed company). Asian Development Bank, Shinsei Bank Limited (from Japan) and Sino Finance Group Company Limited, U.S.A.

Based in Xiamen, XIB operates Xiamen International Investment Limited (XIIL) in Hong Kong and Luso International Banking Limited (LIB) which runs ten branches in Macau. Both are subsidiaries of XIB. In Chinese Mainland, XIB operates six establishments including Shanghai Branch, Fuzhou Branch, Zhuhai Branch, Xiamen Sub-branch and Quanzhou Representative Office. The construction of Fuzhou Gulou Sub-branch and Xiamen Jiahe Sub-branch is also well underway. So far, the Bank has established correspondent relationships with more than 170 banks from 59 countries and regions, with business links all over the world.

At present, XIB has been officially granted the licenses on full-range foreign exchange business operation and partial RMB business as well as the qualification for financial derivatives dealing. Hence, it is entitled to offer full-range foreign exchange business for personal customers and comprehensive financial services including RMB business for corporate and non-resident customers.

XIB pursues the philosophy of 'managing with wisdom' as its guiding principle, and regards 'recognizing human merits, advocating innovations, development and assertiveness, cultivating the spirit of teamwork and dedication' as the soul of the corporate culture. Contributing to the distinguished operation features of XIB are the marketing strategy of 'sticking to niche market', its strenuous efforts made on building flexible and efficient operation mechanism, elastic organization framework catering to the demands of the customers and markets, its innovative business exploration strategy characterized by portfolio products as well as tailor-made solutions which integrate RMB and foreign exchange business, onshore and offshore business, domestic and international business.

Through decades of development, XIB has grown into a bank operating across Hong Kong, Macau and Chinese Mainland, enjoying good reputation at home and abroad. For several consecutive years, the Bank has been listed among The Banker's "Top 1000 Banks Worldwide" and in 2005, it was ranked 167 among the "Top 200 Asian Banks". The Bank has also earned several honors including the "National Double Excellence Enterprise with Foreign Capital", the "Major Tax-payers" and the "Top 300 Tax-payers of Fujian Province". In the taxpaying credit rating jointly held by Xiamen State Taxation Bureau and Local Taxation Bureau, XIB is rated Grade A Enterprise (the highest grade).

XIB not only dedicates to self-development but also contributes to public welfare. The Bank is highly esteemed for its donations to Xiamen welfare institutions and schools in the poverty region, and for organizing public welfare activities including blood donations and tree planting on a yearly basis. These activities have gained wide recognition from the society. Due to its impressive contribution to the public welfare, the Bank was granted the honor of "Enterprise Active in Public Welfare" by the Fujian Assessment Society, as well as "Civilized Corporation in Xiamen", "Corporation of Excellent Professional Ethic in Xiamen" and "Corporation of Excellent Corporate Culture".

2005年，本行積極把握國內經濟強勁增長的良好環境，以“經營智慧”理念為導向，堅持高質量、高效益、可持續發展的方針，加大特色業務創新，完善營銷管理模式，實施全面風險管理。各項業務保持稳健發展趨勢，存貨款餘額分別比上年底增長50.17%和33.75%，稅後利潤比上年增長41.69%，稅後股東權益收益率達到12.86%，稅後資產收益率達到1.01%；資產質量繼續保持優秀水平，不負資產率下降至0.76%，資本充足率保持在14.58%的較好水平。關溝完成全行第一個五年發展規劃，以良好的業績嘉迎我行二十週年華誕。

人民幣同業折借業務和遠期結售區業務資格的取得，上海分行和廈門直屬支行的相繼開業，福州鼓樓支行與廈門嘉禾支行的獲准警建，極大地拓展了本行的發展空間。記載本位幣由港幣轉換為人民幣、資本公積金轉增股本等重大事項的廢利完成，為本行的發展增添了強勁動力。

本行的一切成績，源於廣大客戶的一貫信稱，全行員工的齊心協力，各家股東的全心同護，以及各級政府、監管部門、社會各界的關心支持。在此，我達代表董事會表示由衷的感謝！

“躍馬緊再當路，繼往開來趙新篇”，本行“二五”規劃即將全面實施，新一輪發展的序幕正在開啟！全行員工將以抵爭朝夕和勇於拼搏的精神，扎實工作，乘勢而上，打造廈門國際銀行更加輝煌燦爛的明天。

陳桂宗

In 2005, taking advantage of the favorable environment of strong economic growth in China, and guided by the concept of "managing with wisdom", our bank pursued the principle of "high quality, high efficiency and sustainable development", intensified business innovation, improved marketing management and implemented comprehensive risk management. As a result, all businesses of our bank have maintained good momentum of healthy development. Compared to the figures of last year end, deposits balance and outstanding loans gained an increase of 50.17% and 33.75% respectively. After-tax profits registered a year-on-year increase of 41.69%. Our ROE (after-tax) amounted to 12.86% and ROA (after-tax) reached 1.01%. Meanwhile, our capital adequacy ratio stood at a good level of 14.58%, and our asset quality stayed at an excellent level, with the non-performing asset ratio further reduced to 0.76%. These achievements marked the successful fulfillment of the First Five-Year Plan (2001-2005) of our bank, and enabled us to celebrate our 20th anniversary with fabulous results.

During the year, we obtained the licenses for inter-bank renminbi borrowings and forward settlement of foreign exchange, we opened Shanghai Branch and Xiamen Sub-branch successively, and were permitted to prepare for the establishment of the Fuzhou Gulou Sub-branch and Xiamen Jiahe Sub-branch. All these events have greatly expanded the room for our bank's development. Moreover, it also remarkably motivated our development to realize such achievements as successfully converting our recording currency from HKD into RMB, and completing increase of capital from the reserve.

The achievements made by our bank are results of the consistent trust of our customers, the concerted efforts of our staff, the heartfelt care of our shareholders and the great support of governments and supervisory authorities at all levels as well as the people from all sectors of the society. On behalf of the Board of Directors, I would like to take this opportunity to extend our sincere appreciation.

At the new stage of development, our Second Five-Year Plan is ready for implementation and the curtain covering a new round of development is being lifted. We should go all out for the bright future! The whole staff will seize every minute and opportunity, further consolidate the basis of development, and endeavor to create an even more magnificent future for Xiamen International Bank!
