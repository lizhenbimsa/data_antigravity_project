青海银行党委书记、董事长：

# 董事长致辞

2013年，青海银行在青海省委、省政府的正确领导下，深入践行科学发展观，认真开展党的群众路线教育实践活动，业务经营呈现出良好态势，重点工作硕果累累，开创了改革发展新局面。

经营效益再创佳绩。截至2013年底，全行资产规模491.73亿元，增长34.38%；各项存款余额352.46亿元，增长24.61%；各项贷款余额236.46亿元，增长29.93%；全年实现净利润9.1亿元，比上年增加1.96亿元；各项监管指标全部达标；不良贷款率进一步下降至0.63%，持续实现了“双降”；连续5年跻身“青海企业50强”，连续4年被青海省政府授予“上缴税收大户”荣誉称号，2013年上缴税收达3.8亿元，名列全省金融机构前茅。

信贷投放结构优化。全年累计投放各项贷款432亿元，创历史新高。重点投向了青海省委、省政府支持和引导的支柱产业和优势项目，特别是在支持小微企业发展、农业产业化、化解过剩产能、节能环保产业发展等薄弱环节，以及保障性住房建设、棚户区改造等民生项目方面发挥了地方银行的引领带动作用。此外，还与青海省林业厅、格尔木市政府签订政银合作协议，实现了金融供给与地区经济社会发展对金融需求的有效对接。

小微金融特色彰显。单列小微企业信贷规模，将信贷资源优先向小微企业倾斜，全年累计为822户小微企业提供了融资支持，年底小微企业贷款余额81.64亿元，较上年末增加21.2亿元，增幅高达34.65%，高于全行贷款平均增速4.72个百分点，持续实现了“两个不低于”目标。在青海省内独家引进的德国IPC公司专业微贷技术优势充分显现，规模效应已经形成，在支持小微企业发展方面发挥了独特作用。

机构建设再结硕果。2013年6月，海西州分行开业，同年12月，海北州分行试营业并于2014年2月正式开业运营。目前，青海省内分行已达6家，机构网点覆盖除黄南和果洛外的全省各州、市，提前两年实现了“十二五”机构建设目标。同时，向藏区、县域延伸分支机构，新设共和县、玉树市两家二级支行。



金融创新亮点频出。新一代核心业务系统成功上线，对业务发展的支撑力进一步提升。先后推出公司贷款类产品3个、存款类产品2个，网上银行功能升级，手机银行成功上线，特别是在青海省内率先推出的“如意宝”业务，实现了活期存款享受定期收益，提高了客户收益率，一经推出，便深得客户青睐，广受市场欢迎。

积极践行社会责任。通过存款利率“一浮到顶”、“四免一减”、低利率定价、为青海省红十字会捐款、积极参与“党政军企共建示范村”等各项措施，让利于民，回馈社会。同时，全年共招录290名新员工，其中，异地分行招录全部实现“属地化”，为支持藏区就业发挥了积极作用。

改革发展无有止境。2014年，是青海银行推动战略转型和创新发展的关键一年。我们将在青海省委、省政府的正确领导下，在十八届三中全会精神的有力引导下，认真贯彻落实青海银行2014年工作会议精神，以加快战略转型和创新发展为主线，以推动差异化、特色化、精品化经营为抓手，增强基础管理能力，强化市场拓展意识，提升风险防控水平，为将青海银行建设成为资本雄厚、资产优良、经营持续、内控严密、风险可控、服务优质、形象卓越的现代化区域性股份制商业银行而奋斗不止！

# Chairman's Statement

In the year 2013, under the leadership of the Provincial Party Committee and the Party Government, the Bank of Qinghai fulfills the scientific development outlook, carefully develops the civilian's education and practice by the Party, makes sound achievements in operation, in particular to key work, and opens a new territory for reform and development.

Business benefits hit the record. Till the end of the year 2013, total asset of the Bank is 49.173 billion yuan, increasing by 34.38%; kinds of deposit balance is 35.246 billion yuan, increasing by 24.61%; various loan balance is 23.646 billion yuan, increasing by 29.93%; net profit of the year is 910 million yuan, increasing by 196 million yuan; all supervision indexes are conformed; non-performing load is lowered to 0.63% and amount and ratio are both lowered; the Company is awarded "Top 50 of Qinghai Enterprises" in continuous 5 years, "Key Taxpayer Account" by the Provincial Government for continuous 4 years, with tax paid up to 380 million yuan in 2013, which ranks top in the province.

Optimize credit extension structure. The Company releases various loan of 43.2 billion yuan in total and hits the record. The loan is provided to pillar industries and advantageous projects supported and led by the Provincial Party Committee and the Provincial Government, and the Company plays a leading role as a local bank in supporting development of small and micro-enterprises, agriculture industrialization, dissolving excess production capacity, energy-saving environmental protection industry, indemnificatory housing, shanty town reconstruction and other livelihood projects. Besides, the Company signed government-bank cooperation agreement with the provincial forestry hall and Golmud City and realizes effective cohesion between financial supply and regional economic and social development towards financial demands.

Small and micro-sized financial characteristics take on. Separately list credit loan scales for small-sized and micro-enterprises, incline credit extension loan to small-sized and micro-enterprises, provide financial support for 822 small-sized and micro-enterprises, loan balance of small-sized and micro-enterprises at the end of the year is 8.164 billion yuan, increasing by 2.12 billion yuan compared with the end of the last year, increasing by 34.65%, 4.72% higher than average loan increasing rate of the bank and realized the objective of "not lowering in 2 aspects". As the only one, the Bank introduced professional micro-loan technology by IPC of Germany with advantages emerged and scale effects formed, which plays a unique role in supporting development of small-sized and micro-enterprises.

Body construction achieves fruitful achievements. In June, 2013, Haixi Prefecture Branch was opened, and Haibei Prefecture Branch was in trial operation in December of the same year. At present, there are 6 branches in Qinghai Province, and network covers prefectures and cities except Huangnan and Guoluo and we realize the objective of network construction for the "Twelfth-Five-Year Plan" 2 years in advance. Meanwhile, the Bank extends branches to Tibetan and county areas, and establishes level-2 branches in Gonghe County and Yushu City.



Financial innovation highlights on stage. Successful launching of the new generation of business system improves supporting force of business development. The Company successively released 5 loan products and 5 savings products, upgraded online bank functions, launched mobile phone bank, in particular the "Ruyibao" business which was born as the first one in the province, realized current deposit yielding proceeds of fixed deposit, and increased proceeding rate for clients. This business was much favored by clients and markets after being released.

Actively exercise social responsibilities. Measures like “savings interest up to top”, “four exemptions and one reduction”, pricing at interest rate, donation to the Red Cross Society and participating in “Modal Village jointly constructed by the Party, Government and Military Force”, the Company surrenders profits to the public and repays society. Meanwhile, the Company recruited 290 new employees in the year, all of whom recruited by non-local branches are localized to support employment work in the Tibetan area.

Reform and development are endless. The year 2014 is a key year for the Bank of Qinghai to drive strategic transformation and innovation development. Under the leadership of the Provincial Party Committee and the Provincial Government and powerfully guided by the spirit of 3 $ ^{rd} $ Plenary Session of the 18 $ ^{th} $ CPC Conference, we put the spirit of the working conference of the Bank of Qinghai for 2014, aim to accelerate strategic transformation and innovation development, advance different, characteristic and exquisite operation, reinforce foundation management, intensify market expansion awareness, improve risk prevention and control and strive for build a modern regional stock commercial bank featuring powerful capital, outstanding asset, continuous operation, strict internal control, controllable risk, high-quality service and excellent images.

The Party Committee Secretary and Chairman of Bank of Qinghai: Wang Li

#### 4.1、信用风险

信用风险是指债务人或交易对手未能履行合同规定的义务或信用质量发生变化，影响金融产品价值，从而给债权人或金融产品持有人造成经济损失的风险。本公司面临的信用风险，主要源自贷款组合、投资组合、保证和承诺等。

2013年，本公司风险管理委员会及其下设的专门信用风险管理委员会根据监管要求，在保持合理信贷投放节奏的基础上，不断优化资产结构、有效配置资源，把信贷资源重点向优势领域和重点项目倾斜，优化房地产等行业客户结构。建立了新的授信审批机制，成立流动资金贷款审查、项目贷款评审、个人贷款审查和小企业贷款审查4个中心，加快信贷审查审批流程改造，信用风险管理职能得到进一步强化。同时，注重将信贷资产贷后管理与问题客户风险排查相结合，实现了问题客户常态化、机制化管理，并运用信贷管理系统、现场检查等方式，持续加强对新发放贷款的监测。2013年，本公司信贷结构不断优化，资产质量持续改善，清收处置不良贷款和化解潜在风险贷款工作有效推进，全行信用风险总体呈下降趋势。

#### 4.2、市场风险

市场风险是指因市场价格(利率、汇率、股票价格和商品价格)的不利变动而使银行表内和表外业务发生损失的风险。

流动性风险是指银行虽然有清偿能力，但无法及时获得充足资金或无法以合理成本及时获得充足资金以应对资产增长或支付到期债务的风险。本公司流动性风险主要来自于为贷款、交易、投资等活动提供资金以及对流动性资金头寸的管理。

2013年，本公司进一步完善了流动性管理体系，制定了审慎的流动性管理政策和流动性风险应急处置预案，定期审核和检查全行流动性情况，重点强化日常流动性缺口和流动性储备管理。继续优化资产负债期限结构，控制中长期贷款的增量，积极增加长期主动负债，控制期限缺口；设立了包括法定准备金、超额准备金、同业授信、随时可变现债券等分层次的较为充裕的流动性储备，制定流动性压力测试方案，定期组织进行流动性压力测试。截至报告期末，本公司流动性风险管理情况良好，未发生流动性风险事件。

### 4.2、Market risk

The market risk refers to the risk of losses of business in and out of balance sheet due to disadvantageous changes of market price (interest rate, exchange rate, stock price and commodity price).

The market risk management committee of the Company is responsible for overall coordination and decision-making of market risk management of the Bank; review the market risk management, implementation details and other basic rules and regulations; responsible for formulating and realizing correct measures related to market risk management and internal control and review other issues related to market risk management. Year 2013, the Company carefully researched and judged the financial situations, positively tackled changes of interest rate and exchange rate and avoid any probable impacts on business incurred by market changes, active participation in the interbank market at the same time vigorously develop new business, and improved capabilities of forecast and management of market risks.

### 4.3、Operation risks

The operation risks refer to the risk of losses due to imperfection or any defective internal control procedures, staff, system or external events. The Company's main operation risks include information science & technology risks, internal fraud, external fraud, law risks, rule-breaching operation etc.

In the year 2013, the Company revises the Working Procedure for Operation Risk Management Committee, and further complements management duties of the Operation Risk Management Committee. Continue to prevent and control cases, shift focus from afterwards disposal into beforehand prevention, and change negative status into active status; strength the staff's awareness of conformity, and build the first defense line of risk prevention and case.

### 4.4、Liquidity risks

The liquidity risks refer to risks that the Bank is capable to liquidate but fails to obtain sufficient fund or obtains sufficient funds with reasonable costs to tackle asset increasing or pay liabilities due.
