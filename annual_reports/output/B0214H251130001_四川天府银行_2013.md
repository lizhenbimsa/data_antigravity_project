## 2013年度董事会工作概况

2013年董事会共召集召开了2次股东大会，4次董事会会议，11次专门委员会会议。各位董事勤勉履职，严格按照公司《章程》及议事规则的规定积极参与决策，形成了规范高效的决策机制和决策效果。

一是制定了三年发展规划和年度目标任务。在考虑了国内外经济、金融环境变化的各方面影响，结合我行经营特色和竞争优势的前提下，确定了年度经营目标及财务预算。

二是加强了资本管理。董事会审议确定了我行中长期资本规划和上市工作。同时为提升资本充足率水平，保证我行的可持续发展，拓宽资本补充渠道，决策并确定发行减记型二级资本债券。

三是决策了事业部改革。董事会审定通过事业部制改革方案，事业部制的推行，是全行重大的经营体制和机制的改革。

# WORK REPORT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS 董事会工作报告

#### ■ 加大改革创新力度，促进银行稳健发展

改革工作

2013年我们主要进行了具有深刻影响力的四项改革：

第一是事业部制及流程银行改革。事业部制的推行，流程银行的打造，改变了过去我行总分支这种管理体制和经营体制，把总行不仅变成了一个决策中心和管理中心，更重要的是把总行变成了一个经营中心，这是重大的经营体制和机制的改革。

第二是全面风险管理及网格化风控改革。全面风险管理我们提出了很多年，如何落地就是采取网格化风险管理的路径。

第三是放权松绑及提高工作效率的改革。实际上我们及早地响应了党中央的号召，十八大三中全会提出的全面深化各项改革的决定里面就有这一条，放权松绑，减少审批程序。

第四是“领先一步”打造IT支撑系统。这是一项支撑保障性的改革，IT保障作用去年得到了极大的提速，去年网银和银行卡业务大量交易背景下，我行的核心系统能够有效支撑，这得益于去年领先一步的IT改革。我们通过激发科技人员的力量和潜力，把过去完全外包式、采购式的科技研发变成了以自主研发为主的科技研发模式，极大地激发了科研人员的研发能力。

#### 创新工作

第一是产品创新。一是理财业务。我行理财业务得到极大的丰富完善和提升。二是电子银行产品更加丰富，比过去手段更多。三是负债业务和资产业务产品增多。比如制定出很多社区金融服务的负债产品，包括与慈善事业配合社区金融服务的产品；还有资产业务产品，特别是通过渠道业务建设衍生出来的金融产品跟前几年比得到极大的丰富。

第二是渠道创新。一是电子金融渠道。我们研发创新出很多电子金融渠道，比如推出的“熊猫随易收”。二是通道拓展。我们的投行业务、资管业务，特别是通过金融同业的通道拓展确实有了极大改善。

第三是组织架构和业务架构创新。通过改革创新，截止2013年底，我们基本形成了新的组织架构和业务架构。组织架构是事业部制，特别强调条线对全行工作的布局和指导作用；业务架构有两点：一是解决资产业务和负债业务联动的问题，二是资产业务以什么方式去拓展的问题，业务架构已经和过去的分支机构发生了很大变化。

#### 稳健发展

去年银行的工作稳健发展体现在以下几个方面：第一，总资产、净利润、存款、贷款这四个指标，实现了年初提出的以效益为中心着力推进银行转型的目标，总资产增长19.9%，净利润增长了38%。第二，我行的生息负债资金的成本，比2012年降低了0.25个百分点，生息资本降低了，以效益为中心就得到了充分的体现。第三，我行的主要监管指标，除了核心负债依存度之外，其余的全部达标。杠杆率已经达到4%以上，这是个很不错的进步。去年贯彻以效益为中心的工作指导思想，得到了一些初步的显现。

#### 不良贷款管理的主要政策、措施及其结果

#### ☑ 本行在不良贷款管理方面的主要政策及措施

一是按照《南充市商业银行贷款五级分类实施细则》要求，根据风险特征及时调整分类结果，在摸清风险的前提下，按照要求对信贷资产进行准确的风险分类，真实反映信贷资产风险状态，并结合不良贷款的动态变化对分类结果进行调整，确保不良贷款分类结果与其风险特征相适应。

二是结合本行实际情况，修订完善了《不良贷款清收管理及考核办法》，将该项工作制度化，确立了“统一目标、余额控制、按季考核、年终核算、奖惩斗硬”的总体原则。

三是年初对所有不良贷款进行分类、排序，确定清收重点，并以此为依据对下辖各分、支行分别按季下达不良贷款下降计划，任务的完成情况直接与季度、年度经营情况考核挂钩，并要求各分、支行行长、风险执行官和直接管理贷款的客户经理组进行挂点清收，作为岗位责任的重要内容。

四是积极运用法律武器进行清收。针对部分借款人信誉程度不高、还款意愿不强的情况，我行通过法律诉讼渠道进行清收，并且加强对案件立案、判决、执行的各个环节的管理与督促，努力提高诉讼收贷的成功率。

我行2013年累计清收不良贷款4.052万元，均为现金清收，未核销贷款。2013年末，我行不良贷款余额13.846万元，不良贷款率0.47%。

五是本行成立的专门的不良贷款清收小组由有一定的信贷工作经验、法律知识和清收特长的员工及专业律师组成，直接由总行法律合规部管理。下辖各分、支行对形成的不良贷款在规定期限内无法收回的，全部移交总行清收小组统一管理。清收小组在配合、协调各分、支行进行不良贷款清收的同时，充分整合和利用各种关系及资源，对接管的不良贷款进行逐笔清收，专啃“硬骨头”，起到了非常好的清收效果。

# WORK REPORT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

#### Step up reform and innovation to promote sound development of the Bank

## 1. Reform

We carried out four influential reform actions in 2013:

First, the implementation of the divisional system reform and the building of a process bank. The implementation of the divisional system and the building of a process bank changed the Bank's former management system and operation system of Head Office, branches and sub-branches by turning the Head Office into an operating center, on top of a decision-making center and management center. It is a major reform of the Bank's operating system and mechanism.

Second, comprehensive risk management and meshing risk control. We have been talking about comprehensive risk management for years and meshing risk management is the way to do it. Third, decentralization of powers and work efficiency improvement. In fact, we responded to the call of the CPC Central Committee way back. It is one of the decisions put forward by the Third Plenary Session of the Eighteenth CPC Central Committee for deepening various reforms on a comprehensive basis: decentralize the powers and simplify approval procedures.

Fourth, take the lead to build the IT support system. This is a reform of support and guarantee measures. The IT support function was significantly enhanced last year. Thanks to its IT reform last year, the Bank's core system was able to provide effective support in spite of the massive trade volume of online banking and bank card business last year. By exploring the strength and potential of the IT personnel, we switched from the outsourcing and procurement-based R&D model to an independent one, which has greatly boosted the R&D capability of the IT personnel.

## 2. Innovation

First, product innovation. First, wealth management business. The Bank's wealth management business has been significantly diversified and enhanced. Second, electronic banking products have been further diversified. Third, liability and asset products increased. For example, we have designed many liability products to provide community financial services, including the products for charity work and community financial services; we also have asset products, and the financial products derived from channel building have become more diversified than previous years.

Second, channel innovation. First, electronic financial channels. We have developed and created many electronic financial channels, like the "Panda Sui Yi Shou". Second, channel broadening. Our investment banking and asset management business has been greatly improved through the broadening of peer channels.

Third, innovation of organization structure and business structure. We basically formed a new organization structure and business structure by the end of 2013 through reform and innovation. The organization structure is the divisional system, which stresses how the business lines should arrange and guide the Bank's work. Business structure has a two-fold meaning: one is to resolve the interaction problem of asset business and liability business, and the other is about how to develop asset business. The business structure has changed a lot from the former branch and sub-branch based structure.

## 3. Sound development

The Bank's sound development last year is shown as follows: first, the four indicators of total assets, net profit, deposits and loans fulfilled the year beginning's objective of cost-effective business transformation. Total assets increased by 19.9%, Net profit increased by 38%. Second, the cost of our interest-bearing liabilities fell by 0.25 percentage point from 2012. The reduction of interest-bearing capital is a vivid indication of cost-effective business transformation of the Bank. Third, the Bank's major regulatory indicators have met regulatory standard except for the core debt dependency. Leverage ratio has exceeded 4%, which is a remarkable progress. We have achieved initial results from implementing the guideline of cost-effective business transformation last year.

# RISK MANAGEMENT

First, the Bank adjusted the classification of loans in a timely manner in accordance with the Implementation Rules of NCCB for Five-level Loan Classification, the risk characteristics and the dynamic changes of NPLs, classified the risks of credit assets accurately based on thorough analyses on risks to truly reflect the risk status of credit assets, and dynamically adjusted the classification according to changes in NPLs to ensure the NPL classification results matched with the risk characteristics.

##### ☑ Main Policies and Measures for NPL Management

Second, the Bank revised and improved the Measures of NCCB for Management and Assessment of Recovering Non-performing Loans according to actual conditions to institutionalize the recovery of NPLs. It established the overall principle of “setting universal goals, limiting the balance, quarterly assessment, year-end accounting and strict reward and penalty system”.

Major Policies, Measures and Results of NPL Management

Fourth, the Bank actively recovered NPLs through wielding legal weapons. The Bank recovered NPLs to borrowers with low creditworthiness or repayment willing through lodging lawsuits against them, and strengthened the management and supervision of case registration, decision and execution, etc., to improve the success ratio of recovery.

Third, the Bank classified and sorted all NPLs at the beginning of the year to determine the recovery priorities, assigned the tasks of reducing the NPLs to all branches and sub-branches on a quarterly basis, and directly linked their performance in this regard with the quarterly and annual operation assessment. In addition, the Bank required the heads of all branches and sub-branches, their risk officers and customer manager groups directly responsible for loan management to lead the recovery, which is regarded as one important job duty.

Fifth, the Bank established a specialized NPL recovery group consisting of employees and professional lawyers with experience in credit business, law knowledge and recovery expertise. This group is directly subordinate to the Law and Compliance Department of the Head Office. All non-recoverable NPLs of branches and sub-branches within the specified period were transferred to the recovery group of the Head Office for centralized management. While coordinating with all branches and sub-branches in recovering NPLs, the recovery group fully integrated and used all kinds of relations and resources to recover the NPLs transferred from branches and sub-branches one by one, cracked "hard nuts", and achieved very good results.

##### Results of NPL Management

In 2013, the Bank cumulatively recovered NPLs of RMB 40.52 million in cash. As at the end of 2013, the NPL balance reached RMB138.46 million, with an NPL ratio of 0.47%.
