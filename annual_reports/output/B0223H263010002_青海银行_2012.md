青海银行党委书记、董事长：

# 董事长致辞

岁月流金，盛世丰年。2012年，青海银行在青海省委、省政府的坚强领导下，认真践行科学发展观，改革发展呈现了良好态势，业务经营收获了累累硕果。

经营效益再创新佳绩。截至12月末，青海银行资产总额365.92亿元，增长21.15%；各项存款282.84亿元，增长12.47%；各项贷款181.99亿元，增长38.89%；实现净利润7.13亿元，比上年增加3.07亿元。资本充足率19.34%，核心资本充足率18.40%；拨备覆盖率377.17%；不良贷款占比0.86%，比年初下降0.48个百分点。上缴税金2.26亿元，连续两年荣获“全省上缴税收大户”称号。

改革创新迈出新步伐。青海银行海东地区分行开业营运，省内跨区域发展又迈出了坚实的一步；成功引进德国微贷技术，微贷业务长足发展；成立了票据中心，票据业务迈上了专业化、批量化、规模化的轨道；将个人信贷产品统一包装为三大类共20个信贷产品；研发推出了适应市场需求的“精灵卡”等银行卡新产品；资金业务交易品种更加丰富，收入再创新高；网银业务驶入快车道，手机银行业务进入实施阶段；自主设备和POS机具得到进一步推广和应用；国际业务快速发展，业务量不断攀升。

基础建设取得新成效。顺利推进新一代核心业务系统及外围系统建设，信息科技管理水平有效提升；加大人才选拔和干部交流力度，拓宽了广大员工的晋升渠道，员工薪酬福利大幅增长；广泛开展深度宣传，树立了良好的企业品牌形象；完善职代会职能，使员工权益得到最大保障；有效推动企业文化建设，塑造了更加受人尊敬的企业形象。全行风险管控和案件防范能力进一步提高，实现了全年无重大事故、无重大案件、无重大经济损失的既定目标，连续5年保持了案件的零发生。



信贷投放实现新突破。年内累计发放各项贷款239.29亿元，新增50.96亿元，均创历史新高。全力满足了省内重点项目及客户的资金需求。先后与青海省海南州、西宁市政府、青海省海东行署签订政银战略合作协议，实现了金融资本与地区经济发展需求的有效对接。

发展未有穷期，奋进永不言止。2013年，是我们抢抓新机遇，谋求新发展，实现新跨越的关键之年。我们坚信，在十八大精神的指引下，只要我们以科学发展观为统领，坚定信心，把握机遇，认真贯彻落实青海银行2013年工作会议精神，继续以加快战略转型为主线，以精细化管理为抓手，强化市场拓展能力，提升经营管理水平，为全年工作起好步、开好局，就一定能够完成既定奋斗目标，在加快战略转型中提升核心竞争力，实现青海银行加快发展、创新发展、跨越发展的宏伟蓝图。

# Chairman's Statement

We enjoy both the golden time and the prime year. In the year 2012, strongly led by the Provincial Party Committee and Provincial Government, the Bank of Qinghai practiced the outlook of scientific development, the reform and development takes on sound conditions and business operation is fruitful.

Business benefits hit the record. Till the end of December, total asset of the bank is 36.592 billion yuan, increasing by 21.15%; total deposit is 28.284 billion yuan, increasing by 12.47%; total loan is 18.199 billion yuan, increasing by 38.89%; the total net profit is 713 million yuan, increasing by 307 million yuan. The capital adequacy rate is 19.34%, the core capital adequacy is 18.40%; provision coverage rate is 377.17%; the non-performing ratio is 0.86%, decreasing by 0.48% than that at the beginning of the year. The bank paid tax of 226 million yuan and was awarded "major tax payer of the province" in continuous 2 years.

Reform and innovation march towards a new step. Haidong Branch started operation, marking that the in-province trans-regional operation marching towards. Successfully introduced micro-loan technology from Germany and the micro-loan business achieved a leap-forward development; establish the instrument center, and the instrument business is on the track of professionalism, batch-orientation and scale-orientation; integrate the personal credit products into three categories with 20 credit products included; researched and developed "fairy card" and other bank cards adaptable to market demands; the capital business transaction was rich and revenue hit the record; the online bank business is at the fast line, and mobile phone bank business entered the implementation stage; the independent equipment and POS have been further promoted and applied; the international business develops rapidly and business volume was increased significantly.

generation of core business system and peripheral system, and improve information science & technology management; increase the strength of talent selection and carder communication, expand promotion channels for the staff, and increase the staff's remuneration; extensively develop in-depth publicity, and establish sound corporate brand image; perfect the function of the staff delegate conference to maximize the staff's rights and interests; effectively advance corporate culture construction and build up the corporate image of being respected by others. The risk management and control and case prevention capabilities have been improved, realizing the set objective of no significant accidents, cases and significant economic losses throughout the year and zero incurrence in continuous 5 years.



Infrastructure construction reached sound achievements. Smoothly advance construction of a new Credit loan realized new breakthroughs. In the year, the Bank issued loans of 23.929 billion yuan in total, increasing by 5.096 billion yuan, which hit the history. The Bank satisfied fund demands of major projects and accounts in the province and signed government-bank strategic cooperation agreement with Hainan Prefecture, Xining Government and Haidong Administrative Office and realized effective connection between financial capital and local economic development.



Development is indefinite and striving is endless. The year 2013 is a crucial year for us to seize new opportunities, seek new development and realize new leaps. We firmly believe that, strongly led by the spirit of the 18th People's Congress of China and guided by the outlook of scientific development, we are confident, seize opportunities, dedicatedly implement the spirit of working conference of Bank of Qinghai 2013, continues to focus on acceleration of strategic transformation as the main line, improve business management and start with a good beginning. We are bound to finish the set objectives, improve core competitive strength in the strategic transformation and realize the magnificent blueprint of speeding up development, innovative development and leap-forward development.

The Party Committee Secretary and Chairman of

## 2、报告期外部经营环境变化及其影响

### 2.1 宏观经济形势

## 4、本公司面临的风险及相应对策

本公司在经营货币借贷和结算、开展资金营运活动中面临的主要风险包括：信用风险、市场风险、操作风险、流动性风险、信息安全风险和声誉风险等。报告期内，本公司以效益为核心，坚守风险底线不动摇，进一步强化精细化管理；推进“体制、机制、流程、技术、队伍”建设，进一步完善了风险管理的体制和机制；继续推进实施新巴塞尔协议的准备工作，持续深化全面风险管理体系建设。

### 4.1 信用风险

信用风险是指债务人或交易对手未能履行合同规定的义务或信用质量发生变化，影响金融产品价值，从而给债权人或金融产品持有人造成经济损失的风险。本公司面临的信用风险，主要源自贷款组合、投资组合、保证和承诺等。

报告期内，本公司风险管理委员会及其下设的专门信用风险管理委员会根据监管要求，在保持合理信贷投放节奏的基础上，进一步优化资产结构、有效配置资源，努力实现经风险调整后的收益最大化。积极支持经济转型升级中的优势行业，加大对社会事业、农业农村、科技创新、生态环保、资源节约及其他符合国家产业政策支持领域的信贷投放；加大对关系民生、促进消费的内贸流通业和现代服务业支持力度；重点关注融资平台、房地产、钢贸等领域的风险状况，并加强日常风险监控；注重将信贷资产贷后管理与问题客户风险排查相结合，实现了问题客户常态化、机制化管理，并运用信贷管理系统、现场检查等方式，持续加强对新发放贷款的监测。

### 4.2 市场风险

市场风险是指因市场价格（利率、汇率、股票价格和商品价格）的不利变动而使银行表内和表外业务发生损失的风险。本公司由市场风险管理委员会负责全行市场风险管理的整体协调和决策；审议市场风险管理办法、实施细则等基本规章；负责组织制定、落实有关市场风险管理与内控的整改措施；审议市场风险管理其它重要事项。报告期内，本公司认真研判经济金融形势，积极应对利率、汇率变化，审慎管理市场风险，全力规避市场变化对业务经营可能带来的冲击，在信托、票据搭桥等新业务大力拓展的同时，对市场风险的预判和管理能力明显提升。

### 4.3 操作风险

操作风险是指由不完善或有问题的内部控制程序、人员、系统或外部事件所造成损失的风险。本公司面临的操作风险主要包括信息科技风险、内部欺诈、外部欺诈、法律风险、违规操作等。

## 1. 公司治理概况

本公司自成立以来，严格遵守《中华人民共和国公司法》、《中华人民共和国商业银行法》以及《股份制商业银行公司治理指引》等相关法律法规，结合本公司实际情况，不断完善公司治理结构，法人治理结构逐步完善。

### 1.1 完善公司治理基础性制度

报告期内，本公司根据最新监管要求和自身发展需要，不断完善公司治理的基础性制度建设。年初及时组织修订了《青海银行业务授权管理办法》，进一步规范了业务经营活动中的授权管理活动；为了进一步简化授信审批流程，提高审批效率，董事会专门设立了授信审批委员会，并制定了《青海银行董事会授信审批委员会工作细则》；为了强化风险防范能力，提高内控管理水平，适时提出了风险管理报告制度，要求风险主管部门定期上报风险管理情况，及时为高层决策提供有效的信息。
