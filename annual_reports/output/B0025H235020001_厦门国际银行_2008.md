廈門國際銀行成立於1985年，是中國首家中外合資銀行。目前境內股東為中國工商銀行、福建投資企業集團公司和廈門建發集團有限公司，境外股東包括閩信集團有限公司、亞洲開發銀行、日本新生銀行和美國賽諾金融集團有限公司。

廈門國際銀行總行設於廈門經濟特區，在香港、澳門分別擁有附屬機構——廈門國際投資有限公司和澳門國際銀行股份有限公司（下轄10家分行），在北京、上海、福州、珠海、廈門、泉州等地區設有12家營業性機構和1家代表處，同時與遍佈60多個國家和地區的近200家銀行建立了代理行關係，逐步擴大全球化服務網路。

目前，廈門國際銀行已獲得全面外匯業務、對公人民幣業務、衍生產品交易業務等多項業務牌照，可以為個人客戶提供全面外匯業務，為企業客戶和非居民客戶提供包括人民幣業務在内的全面金融服務。

廈門國際銀行堅持“經營智慧”的核心經營理念，奉行“以人為本、創新求變、開拓進取、團結奉獻”的企業文化核心價值觀，堅持國際化、標準化、集約化與精細化的經營管理導向，不斷拓寬戰略創新平臺，加強核心信息系統建設，加快夯實管理基礎；實行“拾遺補缺”的市場策略，打造靈活應變的業務運作機制，建設因應客戶和市場需求的彈性組織體系，通過創新金融產品和個性化方案，大力發展優質的基礎客戶，實現業務的可持續發展；推行全面風險管理，形成科學有效的風險控制體系，及時化解市場突變帶來的潛在風險，確保資產質量保持優秀水平。

廈門國際銀行在實現自身發展的同時，熱心參與各種公益事業回饋社會。積極支持教育事業，設立獎教和助學基金，資助邊遠學校完善教育設施，幫助困難學生完成學業；關心弱勢群體，為福利院配備生活設施，組織向貧困家庭捐款捐物；在全行範圍內開展“5·12汶川大地震”賑災活動，2008年境內外分支機構和員工為支持災區重建家園累計捐贈金額達數百萬元；大力支持環保事業，推行“綠色信貸”，認養數百畝林地，定期組織義務植樹；推廣感恩文化，鼓勵員工自動自發地投身公益事業，踴躍參加志願者行動、無償獻血等各類公益活動。廈門國際銀行在踐行企業社會責任方面做出了積極的努力，得到了社會各界的廣泛讚譽。

作為第一家地跨港澳和大陸的中外合資銀行，廈門國際銀行經過20多年的精耕細作，已經成為一家在國內外享有良好聲譽的銀行集團，連續多年入選英國《銀行家》雜誌評選的“全球銀行1000強”，榮獲全國“外商投資雙優企業”、“企業文化建設優秀單位”、“最佳人力資源典範企業”、地方“納稅大戶”、“利稅100強”、“納稅信用等級A級企業”（最高等級）、“文明單位”等多項榮譽稱號。

Established in August 1985, Xiamen International Bank (XIB) is the first joint venture bank in China. Its shareholders include Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, Fujian Investment and Enterprise Holdings Corporation, Xiamen C&D Corporation Limited, Min Xin Holdings Limited, Asian Development Bank, Shinsei Bank, Limited (Japan) and Sino Finance Group Limited, U. S. A.

Based in Xiamen, XIB operates subsidiaries including Xiamen International Investment Limited (XIIL) in Hong Kong and Luso International Banking Limited (LIB) which runs ten branches in Macau. In Chinese Mainland, XIB operates twelve establishments and one representative office in cities of Beijing, Shanghai, Fuzhou, Zhuhai, Xiamen, Quanzhou, etc. So far, the Bank has established correspondent relationships with nearly 200 banks from more than 60 countries and regions, with its global service network gradually expanding.

At present, XIB has been officially granted the licenses on full-range foreign exchange business operation and corporate RMB business operation as well as the qualification for financial derivatives dealing, which entitle the Bank to offer not only full-range foreign exchange business for personal customers, but also comprehensive business (including RMB business) for corporate customers and offshore personal customers.

XIB pursues the philosophy of "operating intelligence" as its guiding principle, and regards "recognizing human merits, advocating innovations, development and assertiveness, cultivating the spirit of teamwork and dedication" as the soul of the corporate culture. XIB adheres to the orientation of internationalized, standardized, integrated and refined management. It continuously strengthens innovation, enhances the building of core banking system, and accelerates the consolidation of management basis. Contributing to the distinguished operation features of XIB are its market positioning strategy of "sticking to niche market", its efforts made on building flexible and efficient operation mechanism, elastic organization framework catering to the demands of the customers and markets, and the marketing strategy characterized by innovative products as well as tailor-made solutions which build high-quality customer basis, and the comprehensive risk management which features scientific and effective risk control mechanism for timely reducing the potential risks brought by the sudden market changes so as to ensure an outstanding asset quality.

XIB not only dedicates to self-development but also contributes to public welfare. The Bank actively supports education by setting up education funds for teachers and students, and makes donations to schools in the remote areas to improve their school facilities, as well as providing assistance to students in poverty. XIB also cares for the disadvantaged by providing living facilities to Xiamen Welfare Institution, and by donating money or relief materials to poor families. In 2008, XIB institutions both in and outside mainland China and the whole staff of XIB donated millions to the people of the earthquake-stricken areas in Sichuan for rebuilding their homes. XIB energetically advocates environmental protection and "green-credit policy" which restricts loans facilities to high-pollution industries. XIB also adopts several hundred mu of forest land, and organizes tree planting on a regular basis. The culture of "being thankful" is promoted by encouraging the staff to contribute to the public welfare, and to participate in volunteer activities, blood donation, etc. In all, XIB has made active efforts in fulfilling its social responsibilities, and is widely esteemed by the public.

As the first Sino-foreign joint-venture bank operating across Hong Kong, Macau and the Chinese Mainland, through decades of development, XIB has grown into a bank enjoying good reputation home and abroad. For several consecutive years, the Bank has been listed among The Banker's "Top 1000 Banks Worldwide". The Bank has also earned several honors including the "National Double Excellence Enterprise with Foreign Capital", "Excellent Enterprise in Cultural Building", "Best Model Enterprise in Human Resources Management", the local "Major Tax-payers" and the "Top 100 Tax-payers of Fujian Province", "Grade A (the highest grade) Enterprise in Tax-paying Credit Rating", "Model Enterprise in Ethical and Cultural Progress", etc.

2008 年是極不平凡的一年，全球經濟經歷前所未有的“洗禮”。針對國內外紛繁複雜的經濟形勢和國家宏觀調控政策的新變化，本行堅持貫徹“誠信為本、經營智慧、駕馭風險、精藝管理”的戰略思想，靈活應變地採取經營管理策略，著力優化各項結構，全力防範和化解各類風險，大力加強特色業務創新，實現可持續發展。

截至 2008 年末，全行各項業務保持穩健發展，存、貸款總額分別比上年末增長 6.51% 和 6.36%，總資產比上年末增長 2.59%；盈利水平穩步提升，稅後利潤實現平穩增長，稅後股東權益收益率和稅後資產收益率分別達到 16.41% 和 0.85%；資產質量繼續保持優秀水平，不良貸款率和不良資產率分別為 0.84% 和 0.53%。

2008年，本行各項重點工作取得良好進展。加快組織流程變革，完善以客戶為中心的組織架構，為今後實施條線化管理奠定重要基礎；獲准籌建和設立5家同城支行，為深耕本地市場，支持地方經濟建設創造有利條件；設立博士後科研工作站，成為首家設立博士後工作站的外資銀行，對打造銀行戰略創新平臺，提升自主創新能力具有深遠意義。此外，本行進一步加快推進銀行信息系統建設、全面風險管理、股份制改造等各項重大工作，對實現本行中長期發展目標具有重要的戰略意義。



本行的良好發展業績，得益於銀行管理層和全體員工的傾情奉獻，以及各級監管部門、政府部門、廣大客戶和社會各界的支持與厚愛。本人謹代表董事會表示誠摯的感謝！

2009 年，國內外經濟形勢依然嚴峻，制約經濟復蘇的不確定性因素尚未消除。“雄關漫道真如鐵，而今邁步從頭越”，本行將以更加堅定的信念，審慎應對挑戰，敏銳把握機遇，扎實穩健地推進銀行發展，創造明天更輝煌。

二零零九年三月三十一日

The year 2008 has been an extraordinary year, in which the global economy has experienced an unprecedented severe test. To cope with the complex economic environment both home and abroad, as well as the new changes in macroeconomic policies of China, our bank adheres to the strategy of "keeping integrity and wisdom in running business, and adhering to sound risk control and refined management". Flexible management strategies are adopted, our business structures are optimized, and risks effectively prevented or reduced. Innovations in characteristic businesses are strengthened with an aim to realize sustainable development.

By the end of 2008, our bank witnessed sound development in various businesses, with customer deposit and loan outstanding grew by 6.51% and 6.36% respectively compared with the end of the previous year, whereas the total assets rose by 2.59%. The profitability of the Bank grew steadily, with the after-tax ROE and ROA reaching 16.41% and 0.85% respectively. The asset quality was maintained at an excellent level, with non-performing loan ratio and non-performing asset ratio at 0.84% and 0.53% respectively.

In 2008, our bank has propelled various key tasks and achieved favorable progress. Organizational reengineering has been accelerated, through which a customer-oriented organizational structure was improved to lay a sound basis for the future implementation of line management. Five sub-branches have been approved to be prepared or set up, which is favorable for further exploring local markets and better supporting local economies construction. Our bank became the first foreign-invested bank in China to establish a post-doctoral research workstation, which will strengthen our own innovation abilities, and will exert profound influences on the building of a strategic platform for innovation. In addition, various key tasks have been put into full swing, such as information system building, comprehensive risk management, joint-stock restructuring, etc. All these are of strategic significance to the realization of the medium and long-term development goals of our bank.

The achievement of such outstanding performance owes not only to the devotion by the management and the entire staff, but also to the care and support by the regulatory authorities, government bodies, our customers and the public. On behalf of the Board of Directors, I would like to take this opportunity to extend our heartfelt appreciations to them!

In 2009, the economic situation home and abroad remains severe, and uncertainties that restrain economic recovery still linger. With full confidence, our bank will embrace prudently the challenges, grasp firmly the opportunities, and move forward soundly for the even more brilliant future of our bank.

Ding Shi Da

Chairman

31 March 2009

#### 管理層對財務報表的責任

按照企業會計準則的規定編制財務報表是廈門國際銀行管理層的責任。這種責任包括：

(1) 設計、實施和維護與財務報表編制相關的內部控制，以使財務報表不存在由於舞弊或錯誤而導致的重大錯報；

(2) 選擇和運用恰當的會計政策；

(3) 作出合理的會計估計。

#### 註冊會計師的責任

我們的責任是在實施審計工作的基礎上對財務報表發表審計意見。我們按照中國註冊會計師審計準則的規定執行了審計工作。中國註冊會計師審計準則要求我們遵守職業道德規範，計畫和實施審計工作以對財務報表是否不存在重大錯報獲取合理保證。

審計工作涉及實施審計程序，以獲取有關財務報表金額和披露的審計證據。選擇的審計程序取決於註冊會計師的判斷，包括對由於舞弊或錯誤導致的財務報表重大錯報風險的評估。在進行風險評估時，我們考慮與財務報表編制相關的內部控制，以設計恰當的審計程序，但目的並非對內部控制的有效性發表意見。審計工作還包括評價管理層選用會計政策的恰當性和作出會計估計的合理性，以及評價財務報表的總體列報。

我們相信，我們獲取的審計證據是充分、適當的，為發表審計意見提供了基礎。
