### 銀行簡介

廈門國際銀行成立於 1985 年 8 月，是中國首家中外合資銀行，註冊資本人民幣 10.69 億元。現有股東包括中國工商銀行、福建投資企業集團公司、廈門建發集團有限公司、閩信集團有限公司、亞洲開發銀行、日本新生銀行及美國賽諾金融集團有限公司。

廈門國際銀行總行設在中國廈門，在香港、澳門分別擁有附屬機構——廈門國際投資有限公司和澳門國際銀行股份有限公司（下轄11家分行），在北京、上海、福州、珠海、廈門等地設有分行及多家同城支行，在泉州設有代表處。銀行與分佈在60多個國家和地區的近200家中外銀行建立了代理行關係，商務聯繫遍及全球。

目前，廈門國際銀行已經獲得全面外匯業務和人民幣業務牌照以及衍生產品交易業務資格，可以為個人客戶提供全面外匯業務，為企業客戶和非居民客戶提供包括人民幣業務在内的全面金融服務。

廈門國際銀行堅持“經營智慧”的核心經營理念，確立“以人為本、創新求變、開拓進取、團結奉獻”的企業文化核心價值觀。銀行推行“拾遺補缺”的市場營銷策略，打造靈活、高效的業務運作機制，形成因應客戶和市場需求的彈性組織體系，實施以創新為本的業務拓展策略，推出本外幣結合、離在岸結合、境內外結合等各種組合型產品以及個性化方案；實行全面風險管理，形成科學的風險控制體系；以國際化、標準化、集約化與精細化為導向，持續夯實管理基礎。

經過 20 多年的發展，廈門國際銀行已成長為一家地跨港澳和大陸、在國內外享有良好聲譽的銀行集團，連續多年入選英國《銀行家》雜誌評選的“世界 1000 大銀行”和“亞洲 200 大銀行”，多次榮獲全國“外商投資雙優企業”、福建省“納稅大戶”、“利稅 300 強”等榮譽稱號。

廈門國際銀行在自身獲得發展的同時，積極投身各種公益事業回饋社會，培育員工的感恩之心，如組織員工向福利院提供捐贈、參加無償獻血、植樹造林等公益活動，2007年銀行再次捐資百萬支持廈門地區教育事業，得到了社會各界的廣泛讚譽，被福建省評價協會授予“熱心公益企業”榮譽，並榮獲“廈門市文明單位”、“廈門市職工職業道德建設先進集體”、“企業文化建設優秀單位”等稱號。

XIB ANNUAL REPORT 2007

Established in August 1985, Xiamen International Bank (XIB) is the first joint-venture bank in China with a paid-in capital of RMB 1.069 billion. Its shareholders include Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, Fujian Investment and Enterprise Holdings Corporation, Xiamen C&D Corporation Limited, Min Xin Holdings Limited (Hong Kong listed), Asian Development Bank, Shinsei Bank, Limited (from Japan) and Sino Finance Group Limited, U. S. A.

Based in Xiamen, XIB operates subsidiaries including Xiamen International Investment Limited (XIIL) in Hong Kong and Luso International Banking Limited (LIB) which runs eleven branches in Macau. In Chinese Mainland, XIB operates branches and multiple sub-branches in cities including Beijing, Shanghai, Fuzhou, Zhuhai, Xiamen, and a representative office in Quanzhou. So far, the Bank has established correspondent relationships with nearly 200 banks from more than 60 countries and regions, with business links all over the world.

At present, XIB has been officially granted the permission on full-range foreign exchange business operation and partial RMB business operation as well as the qualification for financial derivatives dealing, which entitled the Bank to offer not only full-range foreign exchange business for personal customers, but also comprehensive financial services including RMB business for corporate customers and offshore personal customers.

XIB pursues the philosophy of "operating intelligence" as its guiding principle, and regards "recognizing human merits, advocating innovations, development and assertiveness, cultivating the spirit of teamwork and dedication" as the soul of the corporate culture. Contributing to the distinguished operation features of XIB are its market positioning strategy of "sticking to niche market", its strenuous efforts made on building flexible and efficient operation mechanism, elastic organization framework catering to the demands of the customers and markets, and the marketing strategy characterized by portfolio products as well as tailor-made solutions which integrate RMB and foreign exchange business, onshore and offshore business, domestic and international business. XIB also practices comprehensive risk management with an aim to construct a scientific risk control system, and continuously strengthens management towards the orientation of internationalization, standardization, integration and refinement.

Through decades of development, XIB has grown into a bank operating across Hong Kong, Macau and the Chinese Mainland, enjoying good reputation home and abroad. For several consecutive years, the Bank has been listed among The Banker's "Top 1000 Banks Worldwide" and the "Top 200 Asian Banks". The Bank has also earned several honors including the "National Double Excellence Enterprise with Foreign Capital", the "Major Tax-payers" and the "Top 300 Tax-payers" of Fujian Province.

XIB not only dedicates to self-development but also contributes to public welfare and cultivate the culture of thanksgiving among the staff. The Bank is highly esteemed for the donations to Xiamen Welfare Institution by its staffs, and for organizing public welfare activities including blood donations and tree planting etc. In 2007, the Bank made donation of RMB 1 million to support the cause of education in Xiamen, which received favorable reorganization by the public. Owing to its impressive contribution to the public welfare, the Bank was granted the honor of "Enterprise Active in Public Welfare" by the Fujian Assessment Society, as well as "Civilized Corporation in Xiamen", "Corporation of Excellent Professional Ethic in Xiamen" and "Corporation of Excellent Corporate Culture".

#### 董事長報告

2007年，面對宏觀調控的新形勢和金融市場的新變化，本行審時度勢，靈活應變，及時調整經營策略，強化市場拓展，創新特色業務，各項業務穩健快速發展，盈利水平持續提升，資產質量保持優秀水平。截至2007年末，全行客戶存款、各類貸款餘額分別比上年末增長27.55%和70.07%；總資產比上年末增長39.96%；稅後利潤比上年增長30.04%；稅後股東權益收益率和稅後資產收益率分別達到18.58%和1.02%；不良貸款率和不良資產率進一步下降至0.75%和0.43%。

過去的一年，本行全面推進各項重點工作，取得可喜進展。網點建設取得新突破，北京分行順利開業並在人民大會堂成功舉辦隆重的開業慶典，成為本行全國性營業網點建設的重要里程碑。銀行信息系統建設、股份制改造、組織架構和流程變革、全面風險管理體系建設等各項重大工作全面啟動，對實現本行中長期發展目標具有重要的戰略意義。

優秀業績的取得，浸透著銀行管理層和全體員工的辛勤汗水和無私奉獻，更離不開各級監管部門、政府部門、廣大客戶和社會各界的關心和支持。借此機會，本人謹代表董事會致以由衷的感謝！

2008 年，中國經濟可望繼續保持較快增長的勢頭，但國內外經濟運行的不確定性在增大。“千淘萬漉雖辛苦，吹盡狂沙始到金”，本行將滿懷信心地迎接各種機遇與挑戰，再接再厲，不辜負監管部門、股東、客戶和員工的期望，續寫廈門國際銀行新的篇章。

董事長

二零零八年三月二十七日

In 2007, amid the new environment of macroeconomic control and the changing financial market, based on prudent judgment of the situation, our bank made timely adjustment of our strategies and direction, strengthened marketing and innovated characteristic businesses, thus achieving sound and rapid development in various businesses, along with continuous improvement of the profitability as well as the attainment of excellent asset quality. By the end of 2007, the customer deposit and loans outstanding grew by 27.55% and 70.07% respectively compared with the end of last year, the total assets rose by 39.96%, and the after-tax profit increased by 30.04% against last year. The after-tax ROE and ROA reached 18.58% and 1.02% respectively, while the non-performing loan ratio and non-performing asset ratio decreased by 0.75% and 0.43% respectively.

In the past year, our bank has propelled various key tasks and achieved favorable progress. Breakthroughs were witnessed in branch building, highlighted by the grand opening ceremony of Beijing Branch held in the Great Hall of the People, a landmark in the construction of a nationwide business network. Various key tasks have commenced into full swing which have strategic significance to the implementation of the medium and long-term development of our bank, including information system construction, joint-stock restructuring, organizational and workflow reengineering, etc.

# 審計報告 Report of the Auditors

### 致：廈門國際銀行董事會

我們審計了後附的廈門國際銀行及其合併子公司（以下簡稱“廈門國際銀行”）合併財務報表，包括2007年12月31日合併資產負債表、2007年度合併利潤及利潤分配表、合併現金流量表以及財務報表附註。

## 一、管理層對合併財務報表的責任

按照企業會計準則和中華人民共和國財政部於2001年11月27日印發的《金融企業會計制度》的規定編制合併財務報表是廈門國際銀行管理層的責任。這種責任包括：

(1) 設計、實施和維護與合併財務報表編制相關的內部控制，以使合併財務報表不存在由於舞弊或錯誤而導致的重大錯報；

(2) 選擇和運用恰當的會計政策；

(3) 作出合理的會計估計。

## 二、註冊會計師的責任

我們的責任是在實施審計工作的基礎上對合併財務報表發表審計意見。我們按照中國註冊會計師審計準則的規定執行了審計工作。中國註冊會計師審計準則要求我們遵守職業道德規範，計畫和實施審計工作以對合併財務報表是否不存在重大錯報獲取合理保證。

審計工作涉及實施審計程式，以獲取有關合併財務報表金額和披露的審計證據。選擇的審計程序取決於註冊會計師的判斷，包括對由於舞弊或錯誤導致的合併財務報表重大錯報風險的評估。在進行風險評估時，我們考慮與合併財務報表編制相關的內部控制，以設計恰當的審計程序，但目的並非對內部控制的有效性發表意見。審計工作還包括評價管理層選用會計政策的恰當性和作出會計估計的合理性，以及評價合併財務報表的總體列報。

我們相信，我們獲取的審計證據是充分、適當的，為發表審計意見提供了基礎。

## 三、審計意見

我們認為，廈門國際銀行上述合併財務報表已經按照企業會計準則和《金融企業會計制度》編制，在所有重大方面公允反映了廈門國際銀行2007年12月31日的財務狀況以及2007年度的經營成果和現金流量。
